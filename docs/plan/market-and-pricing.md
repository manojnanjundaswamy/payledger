# Market scan and pricing

What four well-known budgeting apps do, what we take from each, and the two plans PayLedger launches with.
Researched 13 October 2026 from the products' own sites, plus review sites for the prices those sites don't publish.
Prices change: check them again before launch.

## 1. The four apps in one table

| | Goodbudget | Mint (closed) | Monarch | Spendee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Idea** | Envelope budgeting: give every rupee a job before you spend it | Free tracker: link every account, see everything | All-in-one premium money app for individuals, couples and their advisers | Simple, good-looking expense tracker with shared wallets |
| **Best at** | Habit and discipline; couples sharing one budget; saving for big and irregular costs | Zero effort: automatic sync and categories | Net worth and cash flow in one place; couples; goals linked to accounts; recurring-charge detection | Design; shared wallets for couples and flatmates; multi-currency; cash wallets |
| **Bank sync** | Premium only (US) | Yes, 17,000+ institutions | Yes, 13,000+ institutions through several data providers | Yes; crypto and e-wallets too |
| **Platforms** | Web, Android, iPhone | Web, mobile | Web, Android, iPhone | Android, iPhone, web |
| **Business model** | Freemium subscription | Free; paid for by credit-card and loan referrals and ads | Paid subscription only (7-day trial), no ads | Freemium subscription |
| **Price** | Free: 20 envelopes, 1 account, 2 devices, 1 year of history. Premium: $10/month or $80/year (unlimited, 5 devices, 7 years, bank sync) | Free | Core: $14.99/month or $99.99/year; a higher Plus tier was reported in 2026 | Basic free (1 cash wallet, 1 budget); Plus $1.99/month or $14.99/year; Premium $5.99/month or $35.99/year (prices vary by country) |

**Mint is the lesson.** Intuit closed it in 2024 and moved its users to Credit Karma. A free app paid for by selling
financial products to its users lives or dies by the referrals, not by the users' budgets. Monarch grew by being the
paid, ad-free answer to Mint's shutdown. Our plan already says we never sell data and never push financial products
inside the budget; the market says the same.

### What PayLedger has that none of the four has

- **India first**: ₹ with lakh and crore, April–March financial year, payday months, UPI-style categories, Indian
  bank-statement formats in import.
- **Safe to spend that counts bills, savings and set-asides for big yearly costs**, not just budgets.
- **Splitting and budgeting in one ledger** (Phase 5): your share of a group dinner lands in your budget, and the rest
  shows as money owed, not spending. Spendee shares wallets but does not split; Splitwise splits but does not budget.
- **Guests without an account** can join a group by link, which brings new users for free.
- **Imports you can trust**: preview, error report, duplicates skipped, undo the whole import.

## 3. Business model

- **Freemium subscription, no ads, no data selling, no financial-product referrals.** Revenue comes from people who
  want more, not from selling to them.
- **The free plan is a complete budget, not a demo.** Goodbudget's 1-year history limit and Spendee's single budget
  feel like a hostage; our free plan keeps all history and always allows export. Limits apply to *how much* is
  planned, not to seeing your own data.
- **Splitting and guests stay free** (they bring users), as the plan already says.
- **Premium pays for the household.** Couples sharing a budget is what people pay Goodbudget and Monarch for, so the
  household is a Premium feature. A Premium household costs one subscription, not one per member.
- **India pricing.** Monarch's $99.99 a year is about ₹8,500 a year: far too much for India. Our price is set against
  OTT subscriptions (₹149–₹199 a month) and Indian finance apps, and stays under ₹100 a month.
- **Later**: CA / adviser access (B2B, priced per client), bank sync costs per fetch under Account Aggregator, so it
  goes to Premium only.

## 4. The two plans

| | **Free** | **Premium**: ₹99 a month or ₹799 a year (save 33%) |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts, transactions, categories, subcategories, merge | ✓ (up to 5 open accounts) | ✓ unlimited |
| Budgets with status and spending pace | up to 5 categories | unlimited |
| Plan: bills and savings, safe to spend | up to 5 bills and savings | unlimited |
| Annual planner (big yearly costs) | 1 big cost | unlimited |
| Reports, including the April–March financial year | ✓ | ✓ |
| Import (CSV and Excel, preview, undo) | 3 imports a month | unlimited |
| Export (CSV and Excel) and full history | ✓ always | ✓ |
| Groups and splitting with anyone, guests by link (Phase 5) | ✓ unlimited | ✓ unlimited |
| Household: one shared budget (Phase 5) | – | up to 6 members, dependents included |
| Coming next: rollover and "fill budgets from income", net-worth chart, bill calendar | basic | ✓ |
| Coming later: bank sync, statement parsing, insights, CA access | – | ✓ |

- **30-day Premium trial** once per user, started from the app with no card needed. When it ends, nothing is deleted:
  anything over a Free limit stays visible and editable, but nothing new can be added past the limit.
- **Billing** (Phase 6): Razorpay subscriptions (UPI AutoPay and cards, GST invoices). It needs the owner's Razorpay
  account and KYC, so this PR set builds plans, limits and the trial, and the Upgrade button says payments are coming.

### Why these limits

- A new user can run a full month on Free: a bank account, a card, cash and two wallets; budgets for the five
  categories that matter; rent, an EMI and a SIP on the plan.
- Someone budgeting seriously (more than 5 budgets, more than 5 bills, a family) is someone who gets value every day:
  the people who pay for Goodbudget and Monarch.
- Limits only stop adding, never viewing or exporting: nobody is held hostage by their own data.

## 5. What gets built (one PR each)

1. **Plans and limits** (now): Free and Premium on the user, the limits above enforced by core-service with a stable
   `plan-limit` error, a 30-day trial, the plan and usage in Settings, upgrade prompts where a limit is hit, and a
   pricing section on the landing page.
2. **Phase 4.5** (from this scan): budget rollover and "fill budgets from income", net-worth chart, bill calendar
   view, bill suggestions from repeated transactions.
3. **Phase 5**: households and splitting (as planned).
4. **Phase 6**: billing with Razorpay (needs the owner's account), insights, goals, reminders, unreviewed inbox.
