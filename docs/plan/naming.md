# Naming: candidates (nothing decided)

## What the name has to do
- **Fit all three uses**: personal budget, household, splitting with friends. A name that only says "split" or only
  says "expense" boxes the product in.
- **India first, travel later**: easy to say and spell for an English speaker too (Zerodha, Meesho and Khatabook show an
  Indian word can become a strong brand).
- **Feel warm and trustworthy**, not like a bank or a lending app; money in families is emotional.
- **Short**: 2–3 syllables, works as an app icon label, a domain and a verb ("just ___ it").
- **Ownable**: no established fintech with the same name (trademark classes 9 and 36), a free `.com`, `.in` or `.app`
  domain, and free social handles.

## Shortlist
Quick web searches (October 2026) found **no obvious finance product** with these names. That is not a trademark or
domain check: see "Before choosing" below.

| Name | Meaning | Why it fits | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Saajha** (साझा) | "Shared, joint" | Covers households, groups and joint money in one word; warm; uncommon in apps | Spelling varies (Sajha, Saanjha); pick one and buy the variants |
| **Barabar** (बराबर) | "Equal; we're even" ("hum barabar") | Perfect for splitting and settling up; playful, memorable, repeats like a jingle | Says less about budgeting; harder for non-Hindi speakers |
| **Aangan** (आँगन) | The courtyard at the centre of an Indian home | Household-first and family-warm; a "space" metaphor matches personal and household spaces | Says nothing about money by itself; needs a tagline ("Aangan: your family's money, together") |
| **Paisewise** | Hinglish: paisa + wise | Explains itself instantly; India-first tone; works as a verb ("get paisewise") | Feels local, which will matter when going global; "-wise" is common in English app names |

## Considered and set aside
| Name | Why not |
|---|---|
| Gullak (piggy bank) | Gullak Money, a funded Indian gold-savings fintech, already uses it |
| Hissa (share, portion) | Hissa (formerly RuleZero) is an Indian equity and ESOP-management fintech |
| Kosh (treasury) | Kosh (group lending) and Koshex (budgeting) already exist in India |
| Kinfold | An existing family-documents app uses it |
| Hisaab, Bahi, Khata | Generic words used by many apps (e.g. "Bahi Khata" in OkCredit); hard to own as a trademark |
| GharKharch | An existing household expense app |
| Tijori (safe) | Tijori Finance (stock research) |
| Tally | Tally Solutions, the dominant Indian accounting brand |
| Jodo (join, add up) | Strong association with a political campaign |

## Ideas for more options
- Keep the Indian word but pair it with a clear English descriptor in the logo: "Saajha Money", "Aangan Finance".
- Coined English names (e.g. combining *nest*, *kin*, *even*, *share*) travel best, but they need more marketing money
  to mean anything, and the good ones are mostly taken.

## Before choosing
1. Trademark search on the IP India public search (classes 9, 36 and 42).
2. Domains: `.com`, `.in`, `.app`, `.money`; handles on Instagram, X, YouTube and LinkedIn.
3. Play Store and App Store search for the name and close spellings.
4. Say it to 10 people: can they spell it after hearing it once, and what do they think the app does?
