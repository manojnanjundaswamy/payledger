# 15 ideas to make PayLedger better, with a plan for each

Status: **for the owner to review and approve** (written 14 Oct 2026). Each idea has:
- what it solves
- the plan, as PRs
- which plan it belongs to (Free, Premium or Family)
- how much it matters: **Must** (before or right at launch), **Should** (soon after), **Later**

Ideas marked ✅ are built.

**What people pay for:**
- automation: bank sync, receipts, smart categories
- no limits
- sharing with family
- investments and net worth
- an assistant or insights

**Nobody in India combines the three** (budget, split with friends, household) with Indian taxes and statements.

**Proposal: three plans** (prices are the owner's call)

| Plan | Price | Who it's for | Adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | everyone | a complete budget within the limits; splitting with friends always free |
| **Premium** | ₹99/mo or ₹799/yr | one person who wants it automatic | no limits; statement and SMS auto-capture; tax helper; net worth and investments; debt planner; recaps |
| **Family** (new) | ₹149/mo or ₹1,199/yr | a household | Premium for up to 6 people; a shared household budget with roles; contributions; kids' pocket money |

Family is the "top tier": it's what YNAB and Monarch use to justify their price, and it fits India (joint families).

## The 15 ideas

### 1. Bank statement import that recognises the bank — Must, Free (limited) / Premium
- **Problem:** filling a template by hand loses people in the first week.
- **Plan:**
  1. One parser per bank for CSV and Excel exports: HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis and Kotak first; others follow from samples.
  2. PDF statements with the password the bank sets (used once, never stored).
  3. Duplicate detection against the existing import references.
  4. Category guesses (see 3).
- **Needs from the owner:** a few anonymised statements per bank.

### 2. Paste or share bank SMS — Must, Premium
- **Problem:** most Indian transactions show up as an SMS.
- **Plan:**
  1. Paste many SMS at once; a parser for each bank's alert format; preview; add.
  2. Installable web app (PWA) with a Web Share Target, so "Share → PayLedger" works from the Messages app. No SMS
     permission is needed.

### 3. "To review" list and rules learned from corrections — Must, Free — ✅ guesses and rules built (the "To review" list comes with statement import)
- **Problem:** imported items need a quick check, and the same fix shouldn't be needed twice.
- **Plan:**
  1. A merchant list for India (Swiggy, Zomato, Blinkit, IRCTC, BESCOM, Jio…) and UPI handles.
  2. A "To review" list: swipe to accept.
  3. Every correction offers "always do this", which creates a rule you can see, edit and apply to the past.

### 4. Your share of a group expense counts in your own budget, and UPI settle-up — Must, Free
- **Problem:** after a trip, your budget doesn't show what you actually spent, and settling up means typing UPI ids.
- **Plan:**
  1. Your share books as your expense in your category. What others owe you is a "group receivable" account, so
     it isn't counted as spent.
  2. Settling up moves money between your bank and that receivable.
  3. Each member can add a UPI id, so "Pay ₹600" opens their UPI app (`upi://pay?pa=…&am=…`), and "Request" sends a WhatsApp
     message with the UPI link.

### 5. Households — Should, Family
- **Problem:** couples and families run one budget together. This is Goodbudget's and YNAB's core.
- **Plan:**
  1. A shared space with roles: owner, partner, viewer.
  2. Shared and personal accounts.
  3. A household budget, contributions ("each puts in ₹30,000"), and who paid what.
  4. A Family subscription covering everyone.

### 6. Savings goals with targets — Should, Free (1 goal) / Premium — ✅ built (goals, entries for a goal, on the plan)
- **Problem:** people save for something specific: a trip, Diwali, a phone, an emergency fund.
- **Plan:**
  1. A goal with an amount and a date, giving a monthly target (like YNAB's targets).
  2. A linked account, or money set aside in safe to spend.
  3. Progress shown on the dashboard.
  4. A nudge when you're behind.

### 7. Weekly recap and "month in review" — Should, Free — ✅ weekly recap built (the month-end story is next)
- **Problem:** people forget the app exists, which is the main cause of churn.
- **Plan:**
  1. A Monday email: last week's spending, top categories, bills this week, budgets at risk.
  2. A month-end story in the app: where the money went, against last month, one tip.

### 8. Recurring group expenses and receipt splitting — Should, Free / Premium (receipts)
- **Plan:**
  1. Recurring expenses in a group ("Flat rent ₹45,000 on the 1st, split 3 ways").
  2. A receipt photo: list the items and assign them to people. The first version is typed; OCR comes later.

### 9. A forwarding email address — Should, Premium
- **Problem:** bank alert emails and e-statements already arrive in Gmail.
- **Plan:**
  1. A personal address per user (`you.k3j9@in.payledger.app`); a one-time Gmail filter forwards bank alerts to it.
  2. Inbound email (Amazon SES or Postmark inbound) feeds the same parsers as 1 and 2, and the items land in "To review".
- **Needs:** the product name and domain.

### 10. Net worth and investments — Should, Premium
- **Plan:**
  1. Mutual funds from the CAMS/KFintech consolidated statement (CAS PDF, free from the registrars).
  2. FDs, RDs, PPF and gold as accounts with maturity dates (using the utilities' maths).
  3. The net worth chart includes them.

### 11. Debt payoff planner — Later, Premium
- Cards and loans in one plan: avalanche (highest interest first) against snowball (smallest first).
- Shows the date you'll be debt-free and the interest saved, using the loan calculator's amortisation.

### 12. Tax season helper — Should, Premium (an India-only selling point)
- Tag expenses through the year as 80C, 80D, HRA, home loan interest or donations (80G).
- In March, a summary of deductions and the regime comparison (already in Utilities) with your real numbers.
- Export for your CA: CSV and PDF.
- No filing: that would need licences and liability.

### 13. Subscriptions tracker — Should, Free
- Detect recurring charges (bill suggestions already does half of this).
- A list of subscriptions with their yearly cost, renewal reminders, and "still using this?" nudges.

### 14. Live updates without polling, phone notifications and WhatsApp — Later
- Server-sent events for the bell, instead of polling every 5 s.
- Web Push for an installed app (PWA).
- WhatsApp Business messages for bill reminders (needs a Meta business account, approved templates, a per-message fee:
  see `docs/notifications.md`).

### 15. Payments with Razorpay — Must before charging, Premium/Family
- Subscriptions by UPI AutoPay and cards, a GST invoice each month, and plan changes and refunds in Admin.
- Dunning: when a payment fails, retry, then drop back to Free; nothing is deleted.
- **Needs:** the owner's Razorpay account and KYC, and a decision on GST registration.

## Launch blockers (not ideas: required)

- ✅ **Account deletion and "download all my data":** DPDP Act 2023 rights (Settings → Account).
- **Production basics** (`docs/security.md`): HTTPS, managed Postgres with backups, secrets in a vault, and
  two-factor sign-in for staff.
- **A real sending domain:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC for email.
- **Terms of use, privacy policy and a grievance officer:** required for Indian apps.
