# Five ideas per screen, and the next two to build (October 2026)

Each screen as it is today, five ways to make it more useful day to day. **Bold** marks the ideas picked for the next
two MVPs (end of the page). The rest is a backlog, roughly most useful first on each screen.

## Dashboard
1. A morning card: what changed since yesterday (money in and out, a bill paid itself, a budget crossed 80%).
2. "Can I afford it?" on the dashboard: type an amount, see safe to spend after it and which budget it hits.
3. Widgets per space: switching to a household shows the household's numbers (**Households**).
4. A month-end forecast line on cash flow: where the month will end at today's pace.
5. One-tap widget presets for "Salaried", "Freelancer", "Student", "Family".

## Transactions
1. **Bulk import from a bank statement into To review** (**Statement import**).
2. Split one transaction across categories (₹2,400 at DMart: 1,800 groceries, 600 household).
3. Receipts: attach a photo or PDF; the amount and date read from it.
4. Bulk edit: select many, change category, tag or account at once.
5. "For whom": mark spending for a family member or dependent (**Households**).

## Auto-capture
1. **Statement files (CSV, Excel, PDF) go through the same review list** (**Statement import**).
2. Learn from corrections: a merchant you renamed or recategorised is remembered for the next alert.
3. Card and UPI-app emails (Amazon Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Swiggy, Zomato invoices) parsed too.
4. Auto-add mode for alerts you always accept (same merchant, same account, matched three times).
5. Weekly "unreviewed" nudge in the recap.

## Budgets
1. **Household budgets alongside personal ones** (**Households**).
2. Envelope view: drag money between budgets mid-month.
3. Budget templates by income band (50/30/20 for India: needs, wants, savings).
4. Seasonal budgets: Diwali, school fees in June, insurance in March.
5. A "no-spend days" streak.

## Accounts
1. Net worth over time (a monthly snapshot of every balance).
2. Investments as accounts: mutual funds, PPF, EPF, NPS, FDs with maturity dates.
3. Loans with an amortisation table: principal vs interest left, prepayment what-if.
4. **Shared accounts (joint, house cash) belong to the household** (**Households**).
5. Reconcile: enter the bank's balance, see the difference and the likely missing entries.

## Categories
1. Merge suggestions: two categories used for the same merchants.
2. Category trends: which ones grew most against the last three months.
3. Category groups (Needs / Wants / Savings) for the 50/30/20 view.
4. Icons and colours suggested from the name.
5. Shared categories for a household (**Households**).

## Plan (bills, savings, safe to spend)
1. Subscriptions screen: every recurring charge, yearly cost, price rises, "cancel?" list.
2. Daily services: milk, newspaper, cook, maid; mark missed days, bill calculated.
3. Bills split by household member (**Households**).
4. A 12-month cash calendar: known big months (insurance, school fees, Diwali).
5. Reminders by WhatsApp (when the Business API is set up).

## Goals
1. Goal templates: emergency fund (6× monthly spending), wedding, house down payment, child's education.
2. Round-ups: spare change from each expense to a goal.
3. Shared goals in a household (**Households**).
4. Goal timeline: when you'll reach it at the current pace.
5. Celebrate milestones (25/50/75/100%) with a share card.

## Reports and tax
1. Year in review (April–March): top merchants, biggest month, savings rate, a share card.
2. Compare any two periods side by side.
3. Old vs new tax regime calculator from your own entries.
4. Capital gains from imported broker statements.
5. Household report: per member and per dependent (**Households**).

## Household (today a "coming soon" page)
1. **A household space: members with roles, invited by email** (**Households**).
2. **Shared and personal accounts; personal stays private** (**Households**).
3. **Contributions: who paid how much into shared costs this month** (**Households**).
4. Dependents without a login (children, parents) with their own spending view.
5. A summary a member can share without the details ("₹12,400 on food").

## Groups and People
1. Item-by-item bill splitting (scan a receipt, tap who had what).
2. Recurring group expenses (rent every month).
3. Multi-currency trips with conversion at the day's rate.
4. Group budgets for a trip.
5. Settle up by UPI with confirmation from the payer's side.

## Utilities, Settings, Admin, Onboarding
1. Utilities: SIP calculator with step-up; FD/RD maturity; EMI prepayment saver.
2. Settings: two-factor sign-in, active sessions, sign out everywhere.
3. Admin: feature flags per plan, a broadcast message to all users.
4. Onboarding: "bring your last 3 months" step using statement import (**Statement import**).
5. App: offline quick-add on the phone that syncs when back online.

## The next two MVPs

### 1. Households
The product plan's Phase 5 centrepiece, and the most asked-for thing in Indian family finance: one place for the
family's money, with personal money kept private.

- A household is a space (the data model has had them since V1). Create one, name it, invite by email with a role
  (Partner, Member, Viewer); an invite works for an existing account or after sign-up.
- A space switcher in the top bar: Personal ↔ the household. Every money page (accounts, transactions, budgets,
  plan, goals, reports, auto-capture's target) works on the space you're in, so all existing features work for the
  household at once.
- The household page: members and roles, invites, this month's contributions (who paid how much of the household's
  spending), the household's budget status and accounts.
- Personal accounts never appear in the household; leaving a household keeps its history.
- Done when: two people share a household, both add expenses to its joint account, see the same budget, and each
  one's personal space stays invisible to the other.

### 2. Bank statement import
Auto-capture covers new transactions from now on; most people also have months of history in a statement.

- Upload a CSV, Excel or PDF statement from HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis or Kotak (and a generic layout): the bank and
  columns are detected (date, narration, debit, credit, balance), password-protected PDFs open with the password
  typed once and never stored.
- Every row lands in To review (same guesses: merchant from the narration, category, a twin already entered), so
  one review list serves alerts, SMS and statements; "Add all that look right" handles hundreds at once.
- The account is chosen once for the whole file; opening and closing balance are checked against the rows.
- Done when: a real statement from each of the five banks imports with correct amounts, signs and dates, and
  importing the same file twice adds nothing.
